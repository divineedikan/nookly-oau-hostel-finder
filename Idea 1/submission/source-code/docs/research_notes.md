# Research and evidence notes

## Scope

This project tests whether students benefit from a structured way to compare off-campus accommodation information around OAU. It does **not** assume that finding rooms or agent scams are already a proven major problem. That is a question for field research and user testing.

## Data collection policy

- OSM is used only for candidate accommodation features through Overpass; OSM is incomplete in Nigeria.
- Survey records must come from the student team's own field survey or a landlord contact that agrees to publication.
- Every record needs `source`, `date_collected`, and one of `verified_on_site`, `verified_by_contact`, `from_osm`, or `unverified`.
- Unknown values remain `null`; the app labels them unverified.
- No personal data is stored beyond an agreed business name and contact/WhatsApp link.

## What I could not verify

I could not verify the 72% figure's original methodology or date, a causal rent increase after the policy, a reliable count of off-campus robberies, the safety of any area or hostel, the prevalence of room-finding difficulty, or the prevalence of agent scams. The directory now contains 48 sourced accommodation leads and some reported historical prices. Photos, contacts, room types and field verification remain incomplete; published leads do not confirm rental availability.
